# Threat Percentage

Automatically enables World of Warcraft's built-in numeric threat display whenever you log in or reload your UI.

A tiny addon for players who want threat percentages enabled without having to turn the setting back on manually.

- Supports Retail, Classic Era, Hardcore, Season of Discovery, Burning Crusade Anniversary, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Titan, and Forever.
- No configuration, slash commands, or saved variables.
- Uses Blizzard's built-in threat display.

Install it for your chosen WoW client and enable **Threat Percentage** in the addon list. The addon enables the numeric threat setting; when numbers appear depends on Blizzard's threat display behavior and your unit frames.

Disabling or uninstalling the addon leaves the setting enabled. To turn it off, run:

`/console threatShowNumeric 0`
